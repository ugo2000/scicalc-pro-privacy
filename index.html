Privacy Policy — SciCalc Pro

Effective date: 2026-11-11  ·  App: SciCalc Pro (package com.scicalcpro.app)
Operator: Beijing Zhuao Science And Technology Co., Ltd.

Short version: SciCalc Pro is a free calculator that works offline. It requires no account and does not ask you for personal information. The app's formulas, calculator and interface run locally on your device. The only third-party service embedded in the app is Google AdMob, which uses an internet connection to display ads and may process limited data to do so. This page explains what is processed and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Data kept on your device only. Your settings, theme preference, language, favourites and calculation history stay in local app storage on your device. They are not transmitted to us, and uninstalling the app removes them.

Data we do not collect. SciCalc Pro has no user accounts, no registration and no analytics SDK. We do not collect or store personal information on our own servers, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

Data processed by Google AdMob. The app displays banner and interstitial ads supplied by Google AdMob (Google LLC). When ads are shown, Google may automatically process:

  • Advertising ID — a resettable identifier used to personalize or limit ads (Android Advertising ID).
  • Device information — device model, manufacturer, OS version, screen size and language settings.
  • Approximate location — derived from network information, used to improve ad relevance.
  • Ad interaction data — views, clicks and the frequency of ad displays.

Permissions. The app requests android.permission.INTERNET and network access solely so that AdMob can load and measure ads. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or telephone permissions. Ads are filtered to be suitable for a general audience.

2. How the Information Is Used

  • To display ads and measure their performance.
  • To comply with advertising and platform policies, and to prevent ad fraud.
  • To keep the app free for all users.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use the data described above for our own profiling or marketing.

3. Third-Party Services

The only third-party SDK in the app is Google AdMob. Google processes the data described above as an independent controller under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google's use of data in partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Ad personalization controls: https://www.google.com/settings/ads
  • You can reset or opt out of the Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Children

SciCalc Pro is intended for a general audience (students, engineers and educators) and is not directed specifically to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where required by applicable law (for example COPPA or GDPR-K), ads shown to such users are limited accordingly. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at the address below.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID, or disable ad personalization, in your device and Google account settings.
  • Clear all locally stored app data by uninstalling the app.
  • Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing (including under the EU GDPR and California CCPA). Contact us using the address below to exercise these rights.

6. Data Retention and Security

We operate no user accounts and retain no personal data of our own. Any data processed by Google AdMob is handled according to Google's retention practices described in its privacy policy. Data stored locally remains on your device under your control.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Effective date" above and published at this same URL.
